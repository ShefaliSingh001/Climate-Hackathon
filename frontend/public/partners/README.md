# Partner logos on the homepage

| File | Organisation | Why it is shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `abn-lookup.webp` | Australian Business Register (ABN Lookup) | Businesses in the dataset are verified against ABN Lookup |
| `accc.webp` | Australian Competition and Consumer Commission | Consumer law guidance on transparent pricing |

**Before any public use:** the ABN Lookup and DCCEEW logos contain the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, which needs permission from the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet. The ACCC logo also needs the ACCC's permission. Showing these logos must not suggest that ResourceX is approved or certified by these organisations. For a public launch, replace the strip with text-only references unless permission is granted.

The supplied images are small (the largest is 1106 px wide). They are shown at 56 px high, which is within their resolution.
